{# ---- Upload workflow ---- #}

Capture a handwritten MoM

Mobile camera multi-page scan
  1. Photograph each handwritten page with the phone camera (JPG / PNG / WebP), or upload a scanned PDF / DOCX.
  2. The server deskews and compresses the pages into a legible archival PDF under 1.5 MB; raw originals are retained in cold storage.
  3. Statutory metadata is attached: meeting body, date, quorum count against the 40% Rule 16 threshold, attendees, and sign-off status.
  4. Uploading again creates v(N+1) with a mandatory change summary -- the prior version is never destroyed.

EXIF GPS metadata is stripped from resident and MoM camera photos; magic-byte MIME verification and a 15 MB cap are enforced (spec 9.1).

Dual sign-off required

Sec. 37(2) requires both the Chairman and the Secretary signature block, and Sec. 37(3) requires confirmation in the succeeding meeting before a resolution is treated as final.
